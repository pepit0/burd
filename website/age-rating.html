Age rating

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Rated 13+

Burd is intended for users age 13 and older. When you create an account, you must confirm that you meet this minimum age and agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Social features

Burd includes social media capabilities. These features let you interact with content shared by other birders, including:

  • A home feed with posts from people you follow
  • Likes, comments, and reposts on sightings
  • Public profiles and discoverable usernames
  • Publishing journal entries to the community feed

Because of these features, Burd is listed on the App Store with a minimum age rating of 13+.

Not for children under 13

Burd is not directed to children under 13 (or a higher age where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has created an account or provided personal data, contact us at info@feath.xyz and we will take appropriate action.

See our Privacy Policy for more on children's privacy and data handling, and our Terms of Service for platform rules.
