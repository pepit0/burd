Safety and moderation

Burd has zero tolerance for objectionable content. You can report posts, comments, and users in the app, and block users to remove their content from your feed immediately. Our moderation team reviews reports within 24 hours and removes violating content or suspends accounts as needed.

  • Report a post: open the post → ⋯ → Report post
  • Report a comment: long-press or use ⋯ on the comment → Report
  • Block a user: profile → ⋯ → Block user

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your Burd account in the app: Profile (gear icon) → Preferences → Account → Delete account. Deletion removes your profile and associated content subject to our Privacy Policy.
